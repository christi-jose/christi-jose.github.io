## Overview
**Duration:** May 2024 – Present  
**Location:** Toronto, Ontario  
**Partners:** Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC), SOCAAR

The Study of Winter Air Pollution in Toronto (SWAPIT) is a collaborative government-academic initiative.

## My Role & Contributions

### Data Interpretation & Analysis
- Synthesized monitoring data from multiple fixed-site and mobile platforms
- Characterized pollutant patterns across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area
- Developed spatial models for population exposure assessment

### Evidence Synthesis
- Prepared evidence-based summaries for decision-makers
- Translated technical findings into policy-relevant language
- Supported briefing materials for government collaborators

### Stakeholder Communication
- Represented research at ECCC Downsview data workshop (February 2025)
- Facilitated knowledge exchange between academic and government scientists
- Contributed to strategic planning for pollution reduction initiatives

### 2023 Wildfire Analysis
- Quantified wildfire smoke impact on local air quality
- Produced analytical summaries supporting municipal climate planning

## Impact
SWAPIT findings directly inform Toronto's winter air quality management strategies and provincial climate action plans.
