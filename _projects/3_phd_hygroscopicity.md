## Overview
**Degree:** Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering  
**Institution:** Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India  
**Duration:** April 2018 – May 2025  
**Advisors:** Prof. Sachin S. Gunthe & Prof. R. RaviKrishna

This PhD research investigated how atmospheric aerosols absorb water vapor—a critical property affecting cloud formation and climate.

## Field Campaign Sites

### Chennai (Semi-urban Coastal)
- Marine boundary layer influenced by Arabian Sea air masses
- High-salt and organic aerosol concentrations

### Munnar (High-altitude, Pristine)
- Western Ghats mountain location at 1,600 m elevation
- Relatively clean air mass baseline

## Instrumentation & Methods

### Advanced Aerosol Measurements
- **MOUDI:** Size-segregated aerosol collection
- **CCN Counter:** Cloud condensation nuclei and hygroscopicity
- **SMPS:** Particle size distribution
- **Aethalometer:** Black carbon and light-absorbing aerosol
- **ACSM:** Real-time chemical composition
- **QCM-D:** Water uptake characteristics (custom setup developed)
